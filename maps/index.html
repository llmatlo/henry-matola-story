His six campaign stars

Henry's European-African-Middle Eastern Campaign Medal carries a silver service star (5 credits) and a bronze service star (1 credit) — six official campaign credits in total, confirmed by the physical medal and devices in the family's possession. Each one ties directly to the maps below.

Stateside

Fort Bragg, NC · Oct 1940 – Oct 1942

Henry and George Kysilka enlist together at Fort Hayes, Columbus, and train with the newly reactivated 47th Infantry Regiment at Fort Bragg — two years of stateside training before shipping out to North Africa. Henry's Oct 13, 1940 enlistment, over a year before Pearl Harbor, earned him the American Defense Service Medal.

North Africa

Operation Torch & Tunisia · Nov 1942 – Jul 1943

Henry's combat debut: the landing at Safi, the push across Algeria and Tunisia, and the fighting at Kasserine Pass, El Guettar, and Bizerte that ended the North African campaign.

D Company's fight through Troina, Cesaro, and Randazzo — fighting the timeline flags as not confirmed to include Henry, who was on detached duty (Quartermaster, then Seventh Army HQ) for at least part of the campaign, rejoining Oct 24.

Europe

England, Normandy, Belgium, Germany · Nov 1943 – Jul 1945

England training, D-Day (Operation Overlord) and the landing at Utah Beach, the wounding near Orglandes, hospitalization at Cirencester, an AWOL/court-martial episode and months in the replacement-depot system while D Company broke out through Operation Cobra and fought on through Belgium and the Siegfried Line — then Henry's return to the 47th for the Battle of the Bulge, the Rhine crossing, and VE Day.
